A _dimension matcher_ is a string, or a [_slice_](g) of strings, that selects one or more values of a [_dimension_](g) using [_glob patterns_](g), negated glob patterns, and comparison operators. Each key of a [_sites matrix_](g) or [_sites complements_](g) is a dimension matcher.
