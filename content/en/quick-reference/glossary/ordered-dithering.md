title: ordered dithering

In image processing, _ordered dithering_ is a dithering method that compares each pixel with the matching value in a small repeating grid, called a threshold map, to choose a color from the palette. Because each pixel is processed independently of its neighbors, this method produces a regular pattern, such as dots or lines, that resembles a halftone print.
