title: time zone offset
params:
  reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC_offset

A _time zone offset_ is the difference between a local time and [_UTC_](g), such as `-07:00` or `+01:00`. An offset alone does not identify a [_time zone_](g) or its abbreviation, because many time zones share the same offset. For example, on July 15, 2023, the `-07:00` offset applied to both `America/Los_Angeles`, abbreviated `PDT`, and `America/Phoenix`, abbreviated `MST`.
