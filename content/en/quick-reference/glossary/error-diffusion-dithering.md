title: error diffusion dithering

In image processing, _error diffusion dithering_ is a dithering method that processes an image one pixel at a time, replacing each pixel with the closest color in the palette and spreading the difference, or error, to neighboring pixels that have not been processed yet. This method produces an irregular pattern that preserves fine detail, and is well suited to photographs.
