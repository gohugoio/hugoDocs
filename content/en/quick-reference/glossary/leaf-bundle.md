---
title: leaf bundle
params:
  reference: /content-management/organization/#leaf-bundles
---

A _leaf bundle_ is a directory that contains an `index.md` file and zero or more [_page resources_](g). The `index.md` file contains the front matter and content of a regular page. Analogous to a physical leaf, a leaf bundle is at the end of a [_branch bundle_](g). It has no descendants.
