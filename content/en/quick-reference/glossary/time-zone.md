A _time zone_ is a geographic area that observes the same local time, identified by an [_IANA_](g) name such as `America/Los_Angeles` or `Europe/Oslo`. A time zone can have more than one [_time zone offset_](g) during the year because of daylight saving time. For example, `America/Los_Angeles` has an offset of `-08:00` in winter and `-07:00` in summer.

  Strings such as `PST` and `CET` are not time zones. They are time zone abbreviations, and the same abbreviation can refer to more than one time zone. For example, `CST` refers to `America/Chicago` with an offset of `-06:00`, and to `Asia/Shanghai` with an offset of `+08:00`.
