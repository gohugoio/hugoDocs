A _version_ is a [_dimension_](g) that represents a specific iteration, release, or lifecycle stage of content. While [_language_](g) focuses on localization and [_role_](g) focuses on audience, the version dimension allows you to maintain multiple states of the same content simultaneously. Name each version with a [semantic version][], such as `v1.2.3`, a shortened semantic version, such as `v1`, or any other name, such as `dev`.

  See also: [_default version_](g).

  [semantic version]: https://semver.org/
