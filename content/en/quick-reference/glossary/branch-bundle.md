---
title: branch bundle
params:
  reference: /content-management/organization/#branch-bundles
---

A _branch bundle_ is the `content` directory, a top-level content directory that is not a [_leaf bundle_](g), or any content directory containing an `_index.md` file. The `_index.md` file contains the front matter and content of a `home`, `section`, `taxonomy`, or `term` page. Analogous to a physical branch, a branch bundle may have descendants including leaf bundles and other branch bundles. A branch bundle may also contain [_page resources_](g) such as images.
