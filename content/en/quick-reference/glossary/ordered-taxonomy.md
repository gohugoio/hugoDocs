---
title: ordered taxonomy
---

Created by invoking the [`Alphabetical`][] or [`ByCount`][] method on a [_taxonomy object_](g), an _ordered taxonomy_ is a [_slice_](g) in which each element is an object that contains the [_term_](g) and a slice of its [_weighted pages_](g).

  [`Alphabetical`]: /methods/taxonomy/alphabetical/
  [`ByCount`]: /methods/taxonomy/bycount/
